Premium Vehicle PPF

Protect What Matters.
Drive With Confidence.

We use and trust BOP PPF — a high performance, self-healing paint protection film. PPF is a clear, durable urethane film designed to protect your vehicle's paint from everyday damage, keeping your car looking new for longer.

  • ProtectsAgainst stone chips, scratches & road debris.
  • UV ResistantPrevents fading and discolouration from the sun.
  • Self-HealingLight scratches disappear with heat.
  • Crystal ClearVirtually invisible finish that enhances your paint.
  • Long LastingEngineered for durability & long term protection.
